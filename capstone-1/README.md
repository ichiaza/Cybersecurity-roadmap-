
AITEE Community Health Center 

 Project Title & Objective
To assess the cybersecurity posture of AITEE Community Health Center, identify vulnerabilities and threats, prioritize critical security risks, and recommend effective security controls to protect patient data and healthcare operations.

 Key Audit Findings
The assessment identified these 3 core Security weaknesses:
Weak/Reused Passwords  :Increases the risk of unauthorized account access.
Poor Wi-Fi Security : Could allow unauthorized users to access the organizations network.
Lack of Employee Security Awareness:
Increases the likelihood of successful phishing and social engineering attacks.
 Top Prioritized 

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Priority |
|------|------------|--------|----------|
| Ransomware Attack | High | High |  CRITICAL |
| Patient Data Breach | High | High |  CRITICAL |
| Phishing.          | High             | High             |  CRITICAL |

 Actionable Recommendations
Immediate Mitigation Roadmap:
1. Implement Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and strong password policies.
  
2 Conduct regular employee security awareness and phishing training.
Maintain regular, secure, and tested backups.

3 Implement encryption and role-based      access controls for patient data.

4 Strengthen Wi-Fi security and network segmentation.

5 Keep software and systems regularly patched and updated.

6 Use endpoint protection on staff laptops and other devices.

7 Physically secure servers and other critical equipment.
